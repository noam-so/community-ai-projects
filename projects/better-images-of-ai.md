## Principles fit

An arrangement rather than a product, coordinated by one named non-profit with an advisory board and volunteer stewards, so decision rights sit with the coordinator rather than with users or the people depicted. A year-long published research basis predates the library, and AI is deliberately not used: the submission policy restricts AI generated work. Everything is CC BY 4.0 and free with no paywall or email capture, which means the library survives the coordinator, and also that artists cannot recall an image once published. Openness is unusually complete; accountability is thin, with no published selection decisions or corrections route.
