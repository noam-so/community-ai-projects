A draft set of community AI principles

  • Community control

    Communities hold a real stake in the tool and decision rights, including the power to change it, pause it, or switch it off.

  • Starts with need

    The problem comes from the community. AI is used where it is the right answer, and the tool is built to fit need and context.

  • Data stewardship

    Community data is held on the community’s terms and never becomes someone else’s asset.

  • Value returns

    Money, data, insight and capability flow back to the community rather than out to a vendor, and nothing traps them in.

  • Open and accountable

    Code, methods and learning are shared openly, and risks, failures and harms are published.

  • Light footprint

    Uses a relatively small and economical approach that does the job well, considering both people and planet.
